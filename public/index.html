Quick answers

Get more accurate results from the start.

How do I get started?
Allow location and camera permissions, then choose a date and time from the timeline. Switch between Map and AR to explore the sun path in different ways.
How do I use AR view?
Point your camera toward the horizon. If the overlay drifts, recalibrate your compass by moving your phone in a slow figure-eight.
How do I find golden or blue hour?
Open Photo Hours to view golden hour, blue hour, and twilight times. Tap a time to jump the sun path to that moment.
How does shadow tracking work?
Place an object on the map and slide the time to see how its shadow changes in length and direction throughout the day.
Why is the sun position slightly off?
Accuracy depends on compass and GPS quality. Try stepping outdoors, turning off battery saver, and recalibrating the compass.
AR view is blank — what should I do?
Check camera permissions and restart the app. If needed, close other camera apps so Sun Finder can access the camera.
