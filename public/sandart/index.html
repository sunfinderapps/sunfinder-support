How to Use SandArt

Quick tips to get the most out of your moving sand picture.

1. Turn or Flip Your Device

SandArt uses your iPhone or iPad motion sensors so sand always falls toward real gravity. Turn or flip your device in your hand to break the air barrier and watch streams of sand pour through rising bubbles. Lay your device flat on a table and the sand rests.

2. Assisted Rotation

Want to watch SandArt while your device rests on a stand or desk? Open More (…) › Settings › Assisted rotation to display on-screen Rotate and Flip buttons so you can turn the frame with a tap.

3. Save Images & 15s Videos

Long-press anywhere on the sand picture (or open More (…) › Share / Save) to save a snapshot to your Photos library or record a 15-second video of the sand flowing.

4. Shuffle, Daily & Photo to Sand

Tap Shuffle to mix a fresh, uniquely numbered sand recipe, or try Today's picture for the shared daily seed. With SandArt Pro, use Photo to sand to turn any photo into flowing sand grains.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about controls, purchases, and privacy.

Why isn't the sand moving?

Just like a physical sand picture, sand settles at the bottom of the frame or pauses when your device is lying flat on a desk. Physically turn or flip your device upright so gravity pulls the sand down through the air bubbles. If you prefer not to rotate your device physically, enable More (…) › Settings › Assisted rotation to use on-screen Rotate and Flip controls.

How do I restore my SandArt Pro purchase?

SandArt Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase (with Family Sharing enabled). To restore it on a new device, open More (…) › Settings › Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the SandArt Pro screen while signed in with the same Apple Account.

What is included in SandArt Pro?

SandArt Pro is a single one-time purchase that unlocks:
• Photo to sand: Transform any photo from your library into flowing sand grains.
• All frames: Round, Hourglass, Wide, and Heart frames (in addition to Classic).
• Palette choice: Pick your favourite colour palette whenever you shuffle.
• Ambient mode: Keeps the screen awake while your device is charging on a desk.
• Watermark-free exports: Save images and 15-second videos without a watermark.

How do I enable or mute the pouring sound and haptics?

Open More (…) › Settings to toggle Sound (a soft pouring hiss that responds to how fast the sand is flowing) and Haptics (subtle texture as slopes slide). Note that Sound respects your device's physical Silent Mode switch.

Does "Photo to sand" upload my photos anywhere?

Never. When you choose a photo using Apple's system photo picker, SandArt converts that single photo into sand grains directly on your device. SandArt has no servers, no analytics, and no internet connection for your content.

Why does SandArt ask for Photos permission when saving?

When you tap Save Image or save a recorded video, iOS asks for Add Photos Only permission so SandArt can write the new image or video into your Photos library. SandArt cannot read or browse your photo library.
