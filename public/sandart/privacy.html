SandArt Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

SandArt is a moving sand picture for iPhone and iPad. We built it so that it doesn't need your data.

What we collect

Nothing. SandArt has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking or SDKs. We don't receive any information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your device

  • Your pictures and settings. Saved and recent pictures, your settings, and the picture on screen are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Motion sensors. SandArt reads your device's orientation so the sand follows gravity. These readings are used live and never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photos you choose. For Photo to sand, you pick a photo with the system photo picker. SandArt can only see the photo you pick; it's turned into sand on your device and isn't uploaded.
  • Saving to Photos. When you save an image or video, SandArt asks for permission to add to your photo library. It can't read your existing photos.

Purchases

SandArt Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and gives SandArt only a confirmation that Pro is unlocked. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

Children

SandArt is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
